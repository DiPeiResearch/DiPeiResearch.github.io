# Selected projects

<section class="project-card">
  <p class="card-label">NIH-funded research | Randomized trial | Priority populations</p>
  <h2>NICER: Nicotine Intervention and Communication for Empowering Reduction</h2>
  <p>A randomized trial integrating very low nicotine cigarette use with message exposure. The communication component addresses misperceptions and quit motivation among people with serious psychological distress, people with low socioeconomic status, and a general comparison group.</p>
  <p><strong>Methods:</strong> Randomized trial, message testing, qualitative coding, and longitudinal behavioral measurement.</p>
  <p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1186/s13063-025-09216-8">Read the study protocol</a></p>
</section>

<section class="project-card">
  <p class="card-label">NCI-funded fellowship research | Digital intervention | Health equity</p>
  <h2>Menthol smoking cessation and flavored tobacco communication</h2>
  <p>Development and evaluation of digital cessation message for adults who smoke menthol cigarettes and communication about flavored combusted tobacco products, with a focus on people with low socioeconomic status.</p>
  <p><strong>Methods:</strong> Co-creation approach, focus groups, eye-tracking, discrete choice experiments, and a waitlist-controlled randomized trial.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-card">
  <p class="card-label">Principal Investigator | CDC Foundation | Global tobacco control</p>
  <h2>Anti-smoking message themes among young adults in China</h2>
  <p>A multi-wave randomized experiment with more than 1,500 young adults testing anti-smoking video themes and examining psychological distress as a factor in cognitive, emotional, and behavioral responses over time.</p>
  <p><strong>Methods:</strong> Message design, randomized experiment, longitudinal follow-up, content analysis, mediation, and moderation analysis.</p>
</section>

<section class="project-card">
  <p class="card-label">Population evidence | Global Adult Tobacco Survey</p>
  <h2>Anti- and pro-smoking message exposure in China</h2>
  <p>Analysis of 2018 Global Adult Tobacco Survey data examining exposure to pro- and anti-smoking messages across smoking status and its relationship with smoking-related beliefs and policy-relevant outcomes.</p>
  <p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0304028">Read the PLOS ONE article</a></p>
</section>

<section class="project-card">
  <p class="card-label">Cross-national research | 20 countries</p>
  <h2>Reasons adults who smoke do not use e-cigarettes</h2>
  <p>Analysis of Euromonitor International's Voice of the Consumer: Nicotine Survey examining reasons adults who smoke report for not using e-cigarettes across 20 countries.</p>
  <p><a href="https://doi.org/10.1186/s12889-025-25534-7">Read the BMC Public Health article</a></p>
</section>

<section class="project-card">
  <p class="card-label"> Health decisions | Risk communication</p>
  <h2>Risk, uncertainty, emotion, and health decision-making</h2>
  <p>Earlier work examines antibiotic self-medication decisions, cancer information seeking, HPV communication, air-pollution protective behavior, and responses to climate-change health messages. Together, these studies provide a broader communication-science foundation for the current tobacco-focused program.</p>
</section>
